Governance & provenance

How to Interpret NFSS GeoSafe

01

Reports are signals

Submission does not establish that a crime or security event occurred.

02

Sources remain distinct

Authoritative, open, modelled, derived and synthetic sources retain their provenance.

03

GIS supports decisions

Distance, population, building and environmental context support officers but do not replace field verification.

04

Privacy by design

Public GitHub data excludes reporter identities, confidential security records and sensitive operational locations.
